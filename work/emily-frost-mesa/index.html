Emily Frost has worked in skin health and aesthetics since 2015: first as a nurse injector and laser specialist, then for years beside a plastic surgeon. In February 2020 she opened Frost Aesthetics in Mesa. Today, other injectors fly in to train with her.

The menu is short on purpose.

Emily does a focused set of treatments, and does them at a level other providers train on. Three categories, no filler on the menu itself.

01 InjectablesBotox, Dysport, Jeuveau, and dermal filler, placed the way Emily teaches it: natural first, always. → 02 Laser & advancedBBL Heroic, Moxi, CO2, Virtue RF, and SkinPen microneedling.
