A permanent fix for missing teeth.

Dental implants look, feel, and work like your natural teeth. No slipping dentures, no grinding down healthy teeth for a bridge. One permanent answer.

Why implants

The closest thing to getting your tooth back

An implant is a small titanium post placed in the jawbone, where it fuses with the bone in a process called osseointegration. A custom crown goes on top. The result is a tooth that is anchored, stable, and yours.

  • Natural look and feel. Shaped and shaded to blend with the teeth around it.
  • Built to last. With proper care, implants can last a lifetime.
  • Protects your bone. Implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss and facial sagging that follow tooth loss.
  • Leaves healthy teeth alone. Unlike a bridge, nothing gets ground down.
  • Eat and speak normally. Full biting force, no adhesives, no slipping.

Every implant plan starts with 3D imaging, so placement is precise down to the millimeter.

The process

Four visits. One new tooth.

1. Consultation and 3D imaging

We assess bone density and map the exact placement with 3D imaging. You leave knowing the full plan and the full cost.

2. Placement

The titanium post is placed precisely where planned. Most patients are surprised how straightforward the visit is.

3. Healing

Over the following weeks the post fuses with your bone, creating a foundation as solid as a natural root.

4. The crown

A custom crown is attached, matched to your surrounding teeth. You walk out with a complete smile.

Good candidates

Missing one tooth, several, or all of them?

Implants work for a single missing tooth, for spans of several teeth, and for full-arch replacement. If you are in good general health, you are likely a candidate. The consultation confirms it.
