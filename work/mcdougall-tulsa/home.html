Meet your dentist

A dentist who studied why patients are afraid, then rebuilt the visit around the answer.

Dr. Hugh M. McDougall, PhD, DDS trained in dentistry at the University of Oklahoma College of Dentistry, where he was elected president of his class. He also holds a Ph.D. in clinical psychology from Oklahoma State University.

He is the only dentist in Oklahoma licensed to practice psychology, and one of fewer than ten dentists nationwide holding both licenses. OU asked him to join its dental faculty to teach patient management skills, and the American Dental Association seated him on the committee behind the national licensure exam.
