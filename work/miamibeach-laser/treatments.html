The menu

Treatments that keep you looking like you.

Four specialties, eighteen years of practice. Michelle will tell you honestly which one fits and which ones you can skip.

Injectables

Botox

Botox softens the appearance of facial wrinkles, with results lasting 3 to 6 months. FDA-approved for frown lines, crow's feet and forehead lines. Also used for lip lines, chin dimpling, gummy smile, neck bands and teeth grinding. You notice smoothing within 2 to 10 days.

  • Frown lines, crow's feet, forehead
  • Lip lines, gummy smile, neck bands
  • Excessive underarm sweating
Injectables

Dermal fillers

Restores volume where time took it, with a conservative hand. Michelle has built a reputation fixing overdone work from other injectors. Clients describe the result the same way: natural, never obvious.

  • Cheeks, lips, under-eyes
  • Correction of previous work
  • Natural-first placement
Laser

Laser skin rejuvenation

The Cutera excel V+ is the gold standard for skin clarity. A dual-wavelength system treating red, brown, purple and blue concerns with more power, precision and comfort than ever. Safe for all skin types.

  • Sun spots, age spots, pigmented lesions
  • Rosacea, facial redness, broken capillaries
  • Acne scars, wrinkles, crepey skin
Tightening

Ultherapy skin tightening

Ultrasound that lifts and tightens skin on the face, neck and chest without surgery. The only non-invasive procedure FDA-approved to lift the neck, under the chin and the brow. New collagen builds over 2 to 3 months.

  • Neck, under-chin, brow lift
  • Lines on the decolletage
  • No downtime, gradual natural lift
