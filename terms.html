Terms of service

Last updated October 22, 2026. The plain version of how working with us works.

What we provide

Revamps Studio builds and operates Harlan, a text based receptionist system for roofing companies. The system answers missed calls by text, holds a short conversation with the caller, books estimates on the company’s calendar, sends alerts to the owner, follows up on quiet quotes, contacts past customers, and sends a short report every Monday.

What stays yours

Your phone number, your calendar, and your customer data belong to you at all times. We build the system and run the engine. If you cancel, the engine switches off, and your number, your calendar, and your customer list stay exactly where they always were: with you.

Your part

  • Provide accurate business details for carrier registration, and keep them current.
  • Approve the wording the system uses before it goes live.
  • Honor opt outs. When a customer replies STOP, the system stops, and that number must not be texted again from any tool.
  • Use the system only for your own customers and callers, in line with carrier rules and the law for business texting.

Registration and timelines

US carriers require every business to register before it can send texts to customers. We prepare the filing and submit it with you. Approval timelines are set by the carriers, not by us. Approval typically takes about two to three weeks. That figure is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Fees

Your setup fee and monthly fee are set out in your service agreement with us before work begins. They do not change unless we agree the change in writing with you first.

Availability

The system depends on phone carriers and third party platforms. We work to keep it running, but we do not guarantee uninterrupted service. If an interruption affects your service, tell us and we will work on it with the providers involved.

Ending the service

Either side can end the service as set out in the service agreement. When it ends, we switch the system off. Your data stays yours, and we will hand over the records we hold for you on request.
