Who We Are

Plumbers you will know by name.

Peregrina Plumbing Company Inc is a family-owned and operated shop in Alexandria, Virginia. We opened our doors in 2023, but our master plumber brings more than 14 years of hands-on experience to every job.

That is the whole idea. Big-company skills, small-shop care. When we come to your home or business, you talk to the same people every time. We explain what is wrong in plain language, agree on a price before we start, and clean up when we are done.

We treat every customer's home or business like it is our own.
