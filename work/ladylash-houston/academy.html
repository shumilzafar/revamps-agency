The Academy · Houston, Texas

Learn the craft from Hop Vu.

A 4 to 5 day lash extension program taught by the artist behind 15,000 sets. Leave with the skills, the kit, and the certificate.

Curriculum

What the 4 days cover.

Not a weekend webinar. Four to five full days of hands-on training, from lash science to getting your first clients.

Foundation

Classic and volume lash extension fundamentals: how natural lashes grow, eye health, and when extensions are right for a client.

Eye-shape design

Reading natural eye shapes and designing sets to match: curls, lengths, and thickness chosen to protect the natural lash.

Mega volume, advanced

Handmade fans from 2D to 15D, the fast application method, and mixing curls and lengths for a custom finish.

Glue & technique

Dipping technique, maximizing adhesive performance, isolation, cleaning, safe removal, and re-lashing returning clients.

Consulting & aftercare

How to consult before and after, handling unhappy clients, and teaching aftercare so sets last.

Getting clients

Marketing yourself: photographing finished work at the right angles and building a book from zero.

Included

You leave with more than notes.

Starter kit

Tweezers, classic and volume lashes, adhesive, and a carrying bag. Everything you need to take your first paying client the week after.

Certificate & community

Graduation certificate and membership in the online study group: ongoing technique updates and support from the trainers.
