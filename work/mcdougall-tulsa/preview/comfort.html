Why this works

Your dentist studied fear as a science.

Before Dr. McDougall ever picked up a drill, he earned a Ph.D. in clinical psychology at Oklahoma State University. He is the only dentist in Oklahoma licensed to practice psychology, and one of fewer than ten dentists in the country holding both licenses.

That is not a line on a bio. It is the operating system of this office. The University of Oklahoma dental school asked him to teach its students how to manage anxious patients. The American Dental Association put him on the committee behind the national licensure exam. Calm is quite literally his field.

What a visit feels like

Five promises, kept every time.

One

You are believed

Tell us what scares you, even if it sounds small. Bad past experience, the sound, the feeling of losing control. We listen first and plan around it.

Two

You set the pace

Nothing happens without your go-ahead. Appointments are scheduled with extra time so nothing ever feels rushed.

Three

Your stop signal works

Raise a hand and everything stops, immediately, no questions asked. You will test this before any work begins, so you know it is real.

Four

Comfort is engineered in

Sedation options for any appointment, profound local anesthesia, soft tissue lasers, and digital imaging that shortens time in the chair.

Five

You leave calmer than you arrived

That is the actual goal of every visit here. Not just treated teeth, but proof that the dentist does not have to be the thing you dread.

Always

No lectures, ever

Been away for five years? Ten? You will not hear a word of shame about it. You will hear a plan for what comes next.

Fair questions

From patients who felt like you.

I have not seen a dentist in years. Will I be judged?+

No. A large share of our patients arrived after years away, many of them afraid. The first visit is conversation and a look, nothing more, unless you ask for more.

What if I panic mid-appointment?+

Then we stop. Your stop signal pauses everything instantly, and we only continue when you say so. Panic is a signal we are trained to handle, not an inconvenience.

Do you offer sedation?+

Yes, sedation options are available for any appointment, from mild calming to deeper sedation for longer treatments. We will walk through the choices at your consult.

Can I just come talk first?+

Please do. Call (918) 742-8775 and say you want to talk before any exam. We do this regularly, and there is no charge for the conversation.
