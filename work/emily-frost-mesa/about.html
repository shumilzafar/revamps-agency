About Emily

The trainer's journey.

How a nurse became the injector other injectors learn from.

Emily Frost, RN, has worked in skin health and aesthetics since 2015. She began as a nurse injector and laser specialist, then spent years beside a plastic surgeon, where the standards are exacting and the faces are high-stakes.

In February 2020 she opened Frost Aesthetics in Mesa: one chair, her name on the door, her standards in every appointment. She kept collecting the credentials that matter. Sciton Luminary. SkinPen Ambassador. ZO Skin Health Expert, trained by Dr. Obagi himself.

Today she is a national trainer for Galderma. The injectors learning from her fly in from all over the country. Her clients in Mesa get the same hands, the same judgment, and the same natural-first philosophy she teaches.
