Why it wins patients

Every change, and the reason behind it.

01

The popup is gone

Their site opens with an insurance popup covering the headline. The rebuild opens with a cinematic video, one headline, and one ask. First impressions should not need a close button.

02

Five pages, not one

The old site is a single scrolling page. The rebuild gives implants, cosmetic, family care, and the loyalty program their own pages, so Google and patients can actually find them.

03

Pricing, out in the open

Almost no dentist in Idaho publishes prices. The rebuild shows typical ranges for every major treatment with an exact-quote promise. Transparency is the differentiator nobody there is using.

04

The loyalty program gets a page

Their in-house plan for uninsured patients was a popup and a paragraph. It is now a full page explaining coverage, discounts, and the honest math. This is their best retention tool, finally treated like one.

05

Real reviews, verbatim

Four real patient reviews, word for word, with names attached. Including the one where a husband price-shopped their implants against every quality office and they still won.

06

The name is right

Their own site disagrees with itself: the title tag says Dr. Leeb, the page says Dr. Howard. The rebuild uses the correct name everywhere. Details like this are why patients trust a site.
