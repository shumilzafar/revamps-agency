What we fixed

The business is excellent. The website was losing them quote requests.

Working quote form

The old form displayed an error message directly on the page, and the whole section was duplicated. Quote requests likely never arrived. The rebuild has one clean form that actually sends.

Secure connection

The old site ran on plain HTTP, which browsers flag as "Not secure." The rebuild uses HTTPS, so visitors trust the page enough to submit their details.

Reputation up front

74 years in business and 266 five-star reviews were buried. The rebuild puts the 4.9 rating, the family story, and review snippets where visitors see them first.

Clear next step

The old page had no clear path to becoming a customer. The rebuild has one: request a quote or call (615) 543-1761, visible on every screen.
