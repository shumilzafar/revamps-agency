About

She drew portraits.
Now she sculpts faces.

Megan Davies trained as a nurse, thinks like a scientist, and sees like an artist. That combination is why Scottsdale trusts her, and why other injectors train under her.

Megan Davies, MSN, FNP-BC, CANS

Founder & lead injector

Megan began nursing at Cleveland Clinic and earned her master's as a Family Nurse Practitioner. A dermatology rotation with a top Scottsdale dermatologist pulled her into aesthetics in 2013, and she founded DHH Med Spa in 2015.

Today she is a national trainer for Prollenium, a speaker for the American Med Spa Association, medical director for multiple Arizona practices, and founder of the Academy of Aesthetics Scottsdale, where she trains the next generation of injectors.
