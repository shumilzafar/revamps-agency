What we treat

If it hurts, it's urgent enough.

Don't wait to see if it gets better. Infections spread, cracks deepen, and a tooth that could have been saved becomes a tooth that has to go.

  • Severe toothache. We find the cause, not just the symptom, with digital X-rays and 3D imaging.
  • Broken or chipped tooth. Same-day repair that protects what is left and looks natural.
  • Knocked-out tooth. Time matters most here. Keep the tooth moist, get to us fast, and there is a real chance to save it.
  • Abscess and swelling. Infections need treatment, not waiting. We drain, treat, and stop the spread.
  • Lost crown or filling. Quick, comfortable replacement before the exposed tooth takes more damage.
Why us in an emergency

Fast, precise, and calm about it

Precise diagnosis. Intraoral cameras, 3D imaging, and digital X-rays show us exactly what is wrong, fast.

Sedation when you need it. Laughing gas and oral sedation for anyone whose nerves are already shot. Anxiety-free is the goal.

Save first, extract last. We emphasize preserving your natural tooth. Extraction happens only when it is genuinely the best option.

All ages. Adults, teens, and children get the same gentle, family-focused emergency care.
