Worth knowing

Straight answers about cosmetic work.

Will veneers look fake? +

Not when they are done right. We match shape, shade, and translucency to your face and your other teeth. The goal is a smile people admire, not one they can diagnose.

How long does whitening last? +

Professional whitening typically holds for one to three years depending on coffee, tea, and tobacco. Touch-ups keep it going without starting over.

Can I do this in stages for budget reasons? +

Yes, and most patients do. We plan the full smile, then sequence the work so each stage stands on its own while you save for the next.
