Why it wins patients

Every change, and the reason.

PositioningThe headline sells calm, not dentistry

The old hero says the practice name over a stock family. The new hero says “The dentist for people who dread the dentist,” over cinematic calm. It speaks to the exact patient this practice is built for, in the first three seconds.

StoryThe psychology license leads

The single most differentiated fact in Tulsa dentistry was buried on an interior page. The rebuild puts it in the hero subhead, the doctor section, and its own page. Nobody else can claim it, so it should be everywhere.

TrustReal doctors, real faces

Stock strangers are replaced with Dr. McDougall’s and Dr. Reed’s actual photos from the practice site. Patients meet their dentist before they ever call, which is exactly what anxious patients need.

StructureOne page per decision

Smile design, implants, and the comfort approach each get a dedicated page with their own story, steps, and FAQs. A patient researching veneers lands on veneers, not a homepage wall of text.

The signatureA page nobody else has

comfort.html, “Dentistry for people who hate the dentist,” turns the psychology background into a full patient journey: five promises, fear-specific FAQs, and an invitation to just talk first. This page alone is a reason to choose this practice.

ProofVerified ranking, zero invented stars

No star counts were invented. The rebuild cites the verified discovertulsa.net #1 Tulsa dentist ranking with attribution, and the walkthrough flags the site’s conflicting hours as homework for the practice to confirm.
