Can you get in without damaging my door?

Yes, where possible we use non-destructive entry. We’ll always try picking / bypass methods first and only drill as a last resort, and we’ll tell you before we do.

I’ve lost my car keys, can you help?

Yes. Call 0151 733 6100 with your make and model. We can cut and programme a new key at your location and delete the lost key from the immobiliser so it can’t be used to start your car.

Do you cut spare keys while I wait?

Most standard and Ultion keys are cut while you wait in-store on Westgate Road. High-security keys cut to code can also be posted via Royal Mail First Class if you’re further afield.
