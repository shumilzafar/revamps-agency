Our Coffee

From Farm to Cup

Every bag tells the story of the farmer who grew it.

We buy direct from smallholder farms we know by name, paying above fair trade rates for lots we cup and choose ourselves. Everything we serve is single origin, so what is in your cup tastes like exactly where it grew.

Our menu rotates with the seasons, three origins at a time, and every batch is roasted in East London less than a week before it hits the bar.

About

Opened with One Machine and a Big Idea

Groundwork started in 2019 when Jamie Walsh quit his accounting job to open the cafe he always wished existed. One secondhand espresso machine, a borrowed oven, and a stubborn belief that Dalston deserved better coffee.

Seven years on, the machine has been upgraded but not much else has changed. Same room, same regulars, same idea: good coffee, made with care, in a place that feels like yours.
