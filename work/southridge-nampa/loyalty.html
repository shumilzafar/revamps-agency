Why we publish this

Because surprise bills are why people avoid the dentist.

Fear of the unknown cost keeps more people out of dental chairs than fear of pain. We would rather you walk in knowing the range than stay home guessing. That is the whole philosophy behind the Loyalty Program too: make the money part boring, so the care part can be good.
