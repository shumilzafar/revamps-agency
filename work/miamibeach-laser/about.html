Michelle O'Neill, PA-C

The practitioner

Eighteen years. One philosophy: less is more.

With so many advances in aesthetic medicine, we can love the skin we are in for years to come. My less-is-more philosophy means you rejuvenate and restore, whether with laser tightening, injectables or skincare, and still look like yourself.

Michelle O'Neill is a Certified Physician Assistant who founded Miami Beach Laser & Aesthetics to do aesthetic medicine the way she believed it should be done: conservatively, honestly, and with an artist's eye. Clients fly in from New York. Others have stayed for over a decade.

She is known for three things: telling you what you don't need, fixing overdone work from other injectors, and results so natural no one can tell.
