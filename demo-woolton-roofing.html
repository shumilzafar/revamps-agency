The problem this concept fixes

A 1987 company with a website that hides it.

Woolton Roofing has the reviews, the history and the trade. Their current website does not show any of it. Here is what is wrong, and what this concept changes.

Their current site

  • Generic template design, looks like hundreds of other sites
  • Contact email is a personal @icloud.com address
  • Pages end in .htm, a format from the early 2000s
  • Title tag stuffed with keywords, reads as spam to Google
  • Spelling and grammar errors in the service descriptions
  • 191 five star reviews buried on a gallery page
  • No clear call to action above the fold
  • Established 1987 mentioned once, in a bullet list

This concept

  • Distinctive heritage design built around the 1987 story
  • Professional business email presented properly
  • Clean, modern page structure throughout
  • One clear title that search engines and humans understand
  • Careful, professional copy in every section
  • Review count and score in the header, hero and reviews section
  • Call buttons and a free survey form on every screen
  • Heritage is the headline, not a footnote
Why it matters: a homeowner comparing roofers will check the website before calling. A company with 191 reviews at 4.97 should look like the safest choice in Liverpool, not like a template. First impressions decide who gets the call.
