About Diane

The nurse behind the needle.

Twenty years of ICU nursing before she ever picked up a syringe. That is the whole story, really.

Diane Chuprun, RN is a nationally certified cosmetic injector with over twenty years of hospital ICU nursing behind her and more than twelve years of full-time injecting. She is known in Old Town Scottsdale for one thing above all: a conservative hand and results that never look done.

Her focus is facial harmony, patient education, and ethical care. Every product in the room is FDA-approved and bought directly from authorized US manufacturers. The practice runs under a licensed medical director, in full compliance with Arizona regulations.

Her goal is simple, in her own words: help each client look refreshed, confident, and naturally beautiful at every stage of life.

Three decades, one standard.
