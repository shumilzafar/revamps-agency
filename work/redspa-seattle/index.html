The walkthrough

What changed, and why.

01

A concept, not a layout

The old site is a directory: thumbnails in a grid, text in a list. The concept is called STEP INSIDE. Every choice serves one idea: the site should feel like entering a private candlelit world. Intimate, romantic, secret. The interface carries the brand, not just the colors.

02

Motion everywhere

Candlelight video fills the hero. A tilted marquee scrolls the offering. Headlines rise in on load, sections reveal on scroll, cards lift on hover. Nothing on the page sits still, because a spa that sells atmosphere cannot look static.

03

Suites as chapters

Eight themed suites were eight small thumbnails. Now each suite gets a full-screen cinematic chapter: Painter's Studio, Sunset Suite, Moroccan Suite, each with its real description, real features, and a direct path to booking that evening.

04

Real prices, up front

$648 to $1,016, printed plainly next to what each package includes. Their old site makes visitors hunt across suite pages to assemble the price. Transparent pricing is trust, and trust books.

05

Plan Your Escape

The signature interactive piece their site has nothing like: pick the occasion, pick the suite, pick the package, and the builder totals your evening with a call-to-book button. Three choices, sixty seconds, one phone call. Built for retention, built to convert.

06

Their words, their voice

Real reviews, verbatim: "one of the best investments you can make as a couple." Real FAQ answers from their real policies. Copy written confident, sensual, and spare. Never clinical, never generic.

07

One phone number, everywhere

(206) 963-0633 appears in the nav, every booking band, and the footer. The old site routes same-day bookers through a maze. Here the phone is always one glance away.
