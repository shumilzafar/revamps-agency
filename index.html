FAQ

Honest answers.

Why is the website only $150?

We keep it simple. No meetings, no account managers, no bloated process. You get a focused rebuild from people who do this all day. The demo-first model means we only spend time on customers who actually want the work.

What if I do not like the demo?

Then you pay nothing and keep your current site. The demo is genuinely free. We would rather you walk away than pay for something you do not love.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, the site files are yours, and there are no monthly fees to us. If you ever want someone else to manage it, you can take it anywhere.

How does the textback work?

When someone calls and you cannot pick up, our system texts them back within seconds. It asks what they need, collects their name and number, and alerts you. You call them back when you are free. You pay your own phone and SMS costs directly.

How long does it take?

A website demo takes 2 to 3 days. Once approved, the finished site is usually live within a week. Textback setup takes about a week including testing.

What do you need from me?

Your business name, the services you offer, your phone number, and any photos you have. We handle the rest.
