What this redesign fixes

Dry Tech has a 4.96 rating from 100 customers. Their website should say that, not hide it.

Before The current site

  • Two email addresses listed on the same page. Customers guess which one works.
  • Phone numbers jammed together with no spacing, repeated on every page.
  • Long blocks of keyword heavy text that most visitors will not read.
  • No online quote form. The only option is to find the contact page and write an email.
  • Outdated static pages that make a 4.96 rated company look like it stopped trying.

After This concept

  • One email, everywhere. office@drytechroofingsolutions.co.uk, consistent across the site.
  • Clear click to call numbers in the header, hero and footer. Formatted properly, once each.
  • Scannable services and facts. What they do, where they work, why to trust them, in seconds.
  • A quote form on the homepage and at the bottom of the page. Name, number, job. Done.
  • A design that matches the reviews. 100 five star reviews should land on a site that looks five star.
