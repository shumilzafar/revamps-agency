What we do

Roofing work done properly

From a slipped tile to a full new roof. One local firm, one point of contact, no passing the buck.

R

Roof repairs

Leaks, slipped slates, storm damage. We find the fault and fix it right, without selling you a new roof you do not need.

N

Roof replacements

Full strip and re-roof in slate or tile. Tidy scaffolding, tidy site, and a roof built to last decades.

F

Flat roofing

EPDM rubber and felt systems for extensions, garages and outbuildings. Watertight, guaranteed workmanship.

S

Fascias & soffits

Rotted timber replaced with clean uPVC fascias and soffits. Stops the damp, the birds and the draughts.

G

Guttering

New gutters, downpipes and repairs. Sized right for the roof so water goes where it should.

C

Chimney work

Repointing, lead flashing and chimney repairs. The details that stop leaks before they start.
