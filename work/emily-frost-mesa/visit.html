Asked often.

Do I need a consultation before treatment?+

Yes. Every new client starts with a consult so Emily can see your skin, hear what bothers you, and build a plan around your anatomy. It is the reason her results look natural.

How much will it cost?+

Botox typically runs $300-$500 and dermal fillers $600-$800. Those are typical ranges, not a price list. Emily confirms your exact plan at a consult.

Will I still look like myself?+

That is the whole philosophy. Her most-quoted review is from a client whose lips still look like her lips, just even and confident. Natural first, always.

Is there downtime?+

It depends on the treatment. Moxi and the Frost Glow Facial have little to none; CO2 laser needs real recovery time. Emily tells you upfront, before you book.
