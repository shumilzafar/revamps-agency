Every age, every stage

Care that grows with your family

Children, teens, adults, and grandparents all have different needs, and they all get treated in one familiar place. Kids learn that the dentist is nothing to fear. Parents get honest treatment plans. Grandparents get restorative care that holds up.

  • First visits done right. Gentle, patient introductions that make children comfortable from day one.
  • Prevention first. Regular exams, digital diagnostics, and cleanings that stop cavities and gum disease before they start.
  • Whole-health thinking. Oral health connects to overall wellness, so treatment plans consider the full picture.
  • Continuity. The same team follows your family over the years, so nothing gets lost between visits.

The dentists

Meet Dr. Karr and Dr. Hardee.

Dr. Thomas Karr

DDS, Master, Academy of General Dentistry

Born and raised in Amarillo. Texas Tech, then UT Dental School in San Antonio, class of 1984. The only dentist in the Panhandle to serve as president of the Texas Academy of General Dentistry. Nominated Texas Dentist of the Year twice. Son of Dr. Carter Karr, who practiced in Amarillo for 50 years. Dentistry is the family business in the best sense.

Dr. Clint Hardee

DDS, Fellow, Academy of General Dentistry

Panhandle native, born in Lubbock and raised in Dalhart. Texas Tech zoology degree, then his DDS with honors from UT Health Science Center at San Antonio. Patients know him for a steady hand and a straight answer. The kind of dentist families stay with for decades.
