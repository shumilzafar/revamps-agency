Worth knowing

Family dentistry questions, answered.

When should my child first see a dentist? +

Around their first birthday, or when the first tooth appears. Early visits are short and friendly, which is exactly the point: the dentist becomes a normal place, not a scary one.

How does a same-day crown actually work? +

A 3D camera images your tooth, software designs the crown, and a milling machine carves it from a ceramic block while you wait. Then it is fitted and polished, all in one appointment.

Do you see patients without insurance? +

Yes, and we built something for it. Our Patient Loyalty Program is an in-house plan that covers cleanings, exams, and x-rays for one yearly fee, plus discounts on treatments.
