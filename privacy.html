Privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026. Written in plain words, because that is how we do everything else.

Who this covers

Revamps Studio builds and runs Harlan, a text based receptionist system for roofing companies. This policy covers our website, and the texting that happens when you call or message a roofing company that uses Harlan.

What we collect

When you contact a roofing company that uses our system, we collect only what the conversation needs:

  • Your name and phone number.
  • The service address you give us for the estimate.
  • The messages you send, and your description of the problem.
  • The appointment time you choose.

On this website, we collect what you send us when you book a call or write to us: your name, your contact details, and the contents of your message.

How it is used

Your details go to the roofing company you contacted, so it can reply to you, book and run your estimate, and keep a record of the job. The company owns that record. We use the information only to run the system, to show the company its lead log and reports, and to keep the service working.

Text messaging

If you call a company on our system and the call is missed, the system may text you from that company’s number to continue the conversation. Message frequency varies with the conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out, and no more texts will be sent. Reply HELP for help. Your consent to receive texts is not a condition of purchase, and you can always reach the company by phone instead.

What we never do

We never sell your information. We never share your phone number or your message history with anyone for their marketing. Information is shared only with the service providers needed to run the system, such as the phone carriers and the platform providers, and only so the service works.

How long it is kept

Conversation records are kept in the roofing company’s lead log so the company can follow up on your job. You can ask the company, or us, to delete your information, and we will do it.
