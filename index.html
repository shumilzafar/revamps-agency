FAQ

Honest answers.

Why only $199?

No meetings, no account managers, no bloated process. We rebuild from scratch and keep it lean. The free-demo model means we only spend time on people who actually want the work, so we can keep the price low.

What does "built from scratch" mean?

No WordPress themes, no page builders, no AI-generated templates. Every site is hand-built code, designed around your business. That means it loads fast, has no security holes from bloated plugins, and does not look like every other site on the internet.

What if I do not like the demo?

You pay nothing and keep your current site. The demo is genuinely free. We would rather you walk away than pay for something you do not love.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is in your name, the files are yours, and there are no monthly fees to us. Take it anywhere, anytime.

How does the Rescue Bot work?

You miss a call. Within seconds the caller gets a text asking what they need. We capture their name and number and alert you immediately. You call them back when you are free. One saved job pays for the whole system many times over. You pay your own phone and SMS costs directly to the provider.

How long does it take?

Your demo arrives in 2 to 3 days. Once you approve it, the finished site is live within a week. Rescue Bot setup takes about a week including testing.

What do you need from me?

Your business name, the services you offer, and your phone number. Photos help if you have them. We handle everything else.
