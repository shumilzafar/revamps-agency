How it goes

Planned twice, done once.

First

Evaluation & imaging

Digital imaging maps your bone, nerves, and bite. You get a straight answer on whether implants are right for you, and exact pricing before anything is scheduled.

Second

Placement

The implant is placed under local anesthesia, with sedation available. Most patients return to normal routines within a day or two.

Third

Restoration

After healing, your custom crown, bridge, or denture is secured to the implant. You chew, speak, and smile on teeth that feel like yours.

Fair questions

Asked often.

Does implant surgery hurt?+

Most patients report less discomfort than a tooth extraction. The area is fully numbed, sedation is available, and the comfort approach means the visit runs at your pace.

How long does the whole process take?+

Typically a few months from placement to final crown, because the implant needs time to bond with the bone. That patience is what makes the result last decades.

Am I too old for implants?+

Age alone is rarely the deciding factor. Bone health and overall health matter more, and the evaluation answers that precisely with imaging, not guesses.

What will it cost?+

It depends on how many implants and what they support. Cherry 0% APR plans are available, and most major insurance plans contribute. You get exact numbers at the evaluation.
