Meet Harlan

Harlan is the AI receptionist for roofing companies, built by Revamps Studio. It is a name that talks like a person and reads like a firm: the voice your callers meet when nobody in the office can pick up.

What Harlan always does

  • Answers in about four seconds, from your number.
  • Asks the job questions in order, and writes every answer down.
  • Offers only times that are actually open on your calendar.
  • Pages you at once when a job scores hot or urgent.
  • Stops, cleanly and for good, when a caller replies STOP.

What Harlan never does

  • Never quotes a price by text. Pricing needs eyes on the roof.
  • Never claims to be a person when a caller asks directly.
  • Never texts a number that opted out, from any tool.
  • Never books a time your calendar does not actually have open.
  • Never speaks for your company in words you did not approve.

Asked plainly, answered plainly

What is Harlan, exactly?

Harlan is an AI receptionist for roofing companies, and it works by text rather than voice. It sits on your existing number. When a call goes unanswered, it replies by text in about four seconds, holds a short conversation that qualifies the job, books the estimate on your calendar, and alerts you when the job is urgent. It also follows up on quiet quotes and wakes up past customers.

You own the number, the calendar, and the customer list. We build the system and run it.

Will my customers know they are talking to a system?

The first text introduces Harlan by name, as the AI assistant for your company, in words you approved before it went live. Homeowners care that somebody answered and the job moved forward. If a caller asks directly, it answers plainly, and you can read every word of every conversation in your lead log.

How long before it is live on my number?

The build itself takes days. The wait is carrier registration. US networks require every business to register before it can text customers, and approval runs about two to three weeks. We file it with you at the very start, so the clock runs while we build. We would rather give you the real number now than a fast one on a sales call.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. The number on your trucks and your Google listing stays your number, and customers keep dialing it. Calls your crew answers never change. When nobody can answer, the call rolls over to your system line and the conversation happens from there.

What happens with a real emergency at 2 AM?

The system flags emergencies at once: water coming in, an active drip, a tree on the roof. It books the first available slot, gives the caller the practical first moves for the night, and sends you an urgent alert with the whole conversation attached. It keeps the job calm and booked. The human move after that is yours.

What do you need from me to start?

One call to map your hours and your estimate windows. Access to the calendar your estimates live on. Your past customer list, if you want the reactivation run. That is the whole list. We handle the build, the registration paperwork, and the words, with your approval on each.

What does it cost?

A setup figure and a monthly figure, set by your call volume and the size of your customer list. We put the exact number in front of you on the first call, once we can see both. It is one line, not a menu of packages, and it does not change later.

Does it work with the CRM we already use?

The system books into the calendar your office already uses. Every lead is also recorded in a structured sheet that belongs to you: name, address, problem, score, and status, with the full conversation attached. That sheet drops straight into whatever CRM you run, so your customer record stays yours and nothing is trapped inside our system.

What if I cancel?

The engine switches off. Your number, your calendar, and your customer list stay exactly where they always were: with you. We built it that way on purpose. A system you cannot leave is a system you should not buy.
