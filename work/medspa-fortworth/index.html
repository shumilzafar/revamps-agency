Why this wins patients.

Every change below maps to a real flaw in the current site, and a real reason a visitor becomes a caller.

FixedOne number, everywhere

(208) 466-2458 sits on every screen, one tap to call. Most new patients find you on a phone, and every extra step between reading and calling loses them.

FixedProof before promises

4.9 stars from 421 Google reviews now lives in the hero. Your reputation is the single strongest asset the practice has, and the old site buried it below the fold.

FixedBooking with one tap

Call buttons replace the old "contact us today" dead end. Phone-only booking is completely fine when the phone is one tap away at every scroll depth.

FixedCopy about the patient

"Same-day crowns" and "gentle first visits" instead of practice-speak. People search for their problem, not your equipment, so the site now speaks their language.

FixedDoctors with faces

Real photos, real training, real bios for Dr. Squires and Dr. Howard. People choose people, not practices, and stock smiles choose no one.

FixedNothing stale

No dead blog, no 787-day-old pages. A site untouched for two years quietly tells visitors the practice is too, and first impressions do not get a second hearing.

FixedThe name mismatch

The title tag says Dr. Leeb while the page introduces Dr. Howard. Small conflicts like this cost trust with careful patients, so the redesign carries one true roster.
