Fair questions

Asked often.

Will veneers look fake?+

Not here. Dr. McDougall designs veneers to your facial proportions and natural shade range. The goal patients describe afterward is simple: people notice the smile, not the dentistry.

How long do veneers last?+

Porcelain veneers typically last well over a decade with normal care: brushing, flossing, and regular cleanings. We will tell you exactly how to protect the investment.

Does it hurt?+

Most patients are surprised by how little they feel. Appointments run under the same comfort approach as everything else here: your pace, your stop signal, sedation if you want it.

What does it cost?+

It depends on how many teeth and what they need. Cherry 0% APR plans are available, and we will give you exact numbers at the consult before any work is scheduled.
