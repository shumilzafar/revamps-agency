Before and after

What we fixed on their site

The current All Seasons Roofing site buries a strong business under a weak first impression. Here is exactly what we changed and why.

Before

A defensive banner greets every visitor

The top of the site reads “WE ARE NOT COLD CALLERS! YOU CALL US” in capital letters. It is the first thing a potential customer sees, and it reads as confrontational rather than confident.

After

Confidence replaces confrontation

The header now leads with 243+ TrustATrader reviews and 15+ years in the trade. Proof of quality does the persuading. No shouting needed.

Before

Broken icons where contact details should be

The contact block shows empty boxes instead of phone and email icons. The font failed to load, so visitors see broken symbols next to the numbers that matter most.

After

Contact details that actually render

Phone and email are set in plain, readable text with tap to call links. No icon fonts to break, nothing for the visitor to decode.

Before

The same contact block printed twice

All three phone numbers and the email address are duplicated back to back in the footer area. It looks like a copy paste error and makes the page feel unfinished.

After

One clean contact section

Each number appears once, in the right place. The footer is tidy and the quote section carries the full contact details.

Before

No clear way to get in touch

The homepage is cramped and text heavy, with no call button above the fold. A visitor on a phone has to scroll and hunt to find a number.

After

Call to action in the first screen

A call button sits in the header and the hero, plus a call back form. However they prefer to reach out, it takes one tap.

Every fix above is based on the live site at all-seasons-roofing.co.uk as seen in October 2026. Nothing invented, nothing exaggerated.
