FAQ

Fair questions

What if I don’t like the free demo?

Then you don’t pay anything. The demo is free and there is no obligation. We would rather you walk away than pay for something you don’t like.

How long does it take?

The free demo usually takes 2 to 3 days. Once you approve it, the final site is typically live within a week.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once it is paid for, the site is 100% yours. No monthly fees to keep it, no strings attached.

What do I need to provide?

Your business name, phone number, the services you offer, and the area you serve. If you have photos of your work, send those too. We handle the rest.

Why is it only $150?

We keep things simple. No big team, no office, no salespeople. You get a clean, professional site without paying for overhead you don’t need.

Can you update my site later?

Yes. Small text and photo updates are free for the first 30 days after launch. Bigger changes after that are quoted upfront, so there are no surprises.
