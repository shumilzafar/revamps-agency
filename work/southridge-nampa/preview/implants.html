Dental implants

Dentures sit on your gums. Implants become part of you.

Dental implants are not the same as dentures. While dentures rest on the gums and are held in place by suction or denture adhesive, implants fit directly into your jawbone, mimicking regular teeth.

Eat what you want. Laugh without thinking about it. Brush them like normal teeth, because that is effectively what they are now.

How it goes

Three visits. One permanent tooth.

1. The plan

We image the area, talk through your options, and give you an exact price. You decide with the full picture, not half of it.

2. The placement

The implant post is placed and given time to bond with your jawbone. Most patients are surprised how routine it feels.

3. The tooth

A crown made to match your bite and your other teeth goes on top. You leave chewing on it like it was always there.

Do implants hurt? +

Most patients report less discomfort than a tooth extraction. The area is fully numbed, and over-the-counter pain relief covers the recovery for most people.

How long do implants last? +

With normal care, the implant post itself can last decades, often a lifetime. The crown on top may need replacement after 15 to 20 years of chewing.

Am I too old for an implant? +

Age is rarely the deciding factor. What matters is bone density and overall health, which we check at the consultation before recommending anything.
