What we do

Homes and businesses, roofs that hold up

From a leaking shingle to a full commercial replacement. One local crew handles it all, with materials picked for Ontario winters.

SR

Shingle roofing

Asphalt shingle installation and replacement for homes across the GTA. Measured, quoted and installed by our own crews.

FR

Flat roofing

Flat and low-slope roof systems for homes and commercial buildings, installed to drain properly and stay dry.

RP

Roof repair

Leaks, wind damage, worn flashing. Fast repairs that hold, with a clear written quote before any work starts.

Waterproofing and coatings
