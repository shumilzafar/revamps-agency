Good to know

Questions, answered.

How long does a full set take?

Classic sets run about 1 hour 15 minutes; volume and mega volume up to 2 hours 15 minutes. Your stylist confirms the time when you book.

What if a lash comes off early?

Lash correction is covered up to 5 days from your appointment on Lady Lash Pro sets. Call or text and they will make it right.

Do you work on lashes done elsewhere?

Refill pricing applies to Lady Lash Pro work. For outside sets, book a consult first so the stylist can assess them honestly.

Can I book before or after hours?

Yes. Technicians are available for early morning slots from 7 AM. Contact the studio in advance to arrange it.

How do I keep them lasting?

Keep them clean and follow the aftercare your stylist gives you. With proper care and regular refills, extensions last indefinitely.
